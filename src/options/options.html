No Bookmarklets Found

You don't have any bookmarklets in your bookmarks yet. To create one:

  1. Right-click your bookmarks bar and choose Add page
  2. Set the URL to a javascript: URL (e.g. javascript:alert('Hello, world!'))
  3. Give it a name and save

Then come back to this page — your bookmarklet will appear here.

Debug

When enabled, [bm-runner] messages are logged to the service worker console — not the regular page DevTools (F12). To see them, go to chrome://extensions, find Bookmarklet Runner, and click the service worker link on its card. That opens a separate DevTools window where the logs appear.
